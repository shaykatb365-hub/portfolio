02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From visual design to
digital products.

I'm Shaykat — a UI/UX & Product Designer with a design background that started much earlier in visual communication.

For 8+ years, I've worked across graphic design, branding, print, packaging, social media and digital design. That foundation gradually pulled me toward interfaces and, eventually, product design.

What I enjoy most is figuring out how something should work, not just how it should look. I like making sense of messy information, shaping workflows, building structure and then bringing it to life through clear, considered interfaces.

My visual-design background still plays a big role in how I approach products — typography, hierarchy, composition and visual systems are part of the problem, not decoration added at the end.

In short: I like making complicated things feel simple, useful and considered.
